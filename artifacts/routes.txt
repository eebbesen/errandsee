METRO A Line runs north-south along Snelling Avenue — this is the highest-frequency corridor, with the A Line running every 10 minutes during daytime. It has stations at Snelling & Minnehaha, Snelling & Grand, Snelling & Randolph, Snelling & Highland Parkway, and Snelling & St. Clair.
METRO B Line runs east-west along Marshall and Selby Avenues — this is a high-frequency corridor
Route 84 runs north-south along Snelling Avenue
Route 63 runs east-west along Grand Avenue, connecting to the Green Line at Raymond Station and heading east to Sun Ray Transit Center. It's a key crosstown route through the heart of the neighborhood.
Route 74 runs east-west along Randolph Avenue, serving St. Catherine University and continuing east to downtown St. Paul and Sun Ray.
Route 87 runs north-south along Cleveland Avenue, connecting Highland Village to the U of M St. Paul campus and Rosedale. This gives residents a second north-south spine on the western side of the neighborhood.
Route 134 runs along Cretin Avenue (the western boundary), a limited-stop express route connecting Highland to Minneapolis.
Route 38 runs east-west along Ford Parkway / Highland Parkway at the southern edge of the neighborhood, linking Highland Village to Minneapolis via Uptown.
Route 72 runs from the Lexington Avenue light rail station to Selby Avenue, then to Sun Ray via downtown St. Paul
Route 65 runs north-south from Rosedale to Grand Avenue and Dale Street
Green Line light rail runs east-west along University Avenue
